# Measured Paper

The picture is the measurement and nothing else. Every mark on the canvas is a number that exists in the round 13 results file or is computed from its grades, placed by arithmetic, so a reader who opens the evidence folder can find each dot again. There is no illustration and no ornament. Weight comes from position and colour: surviving-lines is drawn in ink-blue and every other arm stays in graphite.

Space is honest. Both axes start at zero and end at the set's maximum, because the totals are far below the maximum on the sealed set and close to it on the public set, and that difference is part of the result. An arm that has runs on only some tasks is drawn as a hollow dot with a footnote. The text under each chart says where another arm scored higher.

The cover carries a different cut of the same data: the per-task means of the sealed set, so that the two tasks where surviving-lines is not highest are visible at thumbnail size. Labels hang from one right edge, numbers sit in tabular columns, and hairlines divide without boxes or shadows. The three typefaces are the portfolio's own: Bricolage Grotesque names things, Newsreader explains them, JetBrains Mono carries numbers.
