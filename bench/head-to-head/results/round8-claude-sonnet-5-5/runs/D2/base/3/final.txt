- The top author owns 17%. Six people are needed to pass 50%.
- Losing the top two would remove about 27% of the live lines. Losing the top three would remove about 37%.
- Counting non-test code only, it is a bit more concentrated. Eric Paris has 17.3%, Alex Ogier 12.5%, Noah Dietz 8.2% and Riobard 7.5%.

**Why the line counts overstate the risk.**
- **Most of the top authors are gone.**
  - Alex Ogier's commits end in 2015, Eric Paris's in 2018, and Tamal Saha's in 2018.
  - Noah Dietz, Jonathan Reyna and Mitch Connors each have a single commit or a short burst, from 2017 to 2019.
  - Their code is therefore already orphaned. Losing them again changes nothing.
- **The people actually maintaining it hold little of the surviving code.**
  - Tomas Aschan has 32 commits, the most since Oct 2024, but doesn't appear in the top authors by surviving lines.
  - Other recent contributors are Sebastiaan van Stijn, Hu Jun, Georges Varouchas, Ethan P., Sam Okafor, and Maximilian Frank.
  - Lena Voss wrote the latest feature (`Require*` helpers, #512, Mar 2026) and holds about 1.2% of lines.
- **The real risk is the maintainer.** If Tomas Aschan left, nobody else has a comparable review history. That matters more than the line counts.
