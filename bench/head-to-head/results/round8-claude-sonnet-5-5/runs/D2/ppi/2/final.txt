- **Project bus factor is 5 (more than 50% of lines).** It takes Paris, Ogier, Dietz, Reyna and Saha to pass 50% (50.7%). Losing any one or two removes at most 27%.

- **14 of 43 non-test files have a single owner with 80% or more of the lines.** Most are small and self-contained:

## The larger risk is inactivity

- **Most top authors are gone.** Over 70% of the surviving lines come from people who haven't committed in years. Ogier, whose design `flag.go` still follows, last committed in 2015.
- **Activity in the last 12 months is thin.** There were 3 commits from Sam Okafor, 1 from Lena Voss and 1 from dependabot. Tomas Aschan merged PRs through 2025-10 but owns only 67 lines.
- **The repo's last commit was 2026-04-08, about 6 months ago.** By the skill's 90-day "active" test, nobody currently counts as active.
- **The unowned, load-bearing parts are `flag.go`, `golangflag.go` and the slice types.** Those are the areas where you will be the one answering questions on PRs.

## Recommendations

1. **Immediate:** Give your closest review attention to `flag.go`, `golangflag.go`, `count.go` and `string_slice.go`. Their authors are gone, so nobody can say why a quirk exists. Use `git log -L` and `git blame` on the line before approving a change that touches them.
2. **Short-term:** For the 14 single-owner files, a short comment explaining intent would help, starting with `time.go`, `func.go` and the `string_to_*` files. They change rarely, so the cost is small.
3. **Ongoing:** Recruit or name one or two co-maintainers, such as Lena Voss or Tomas Aschan. The recent contributors know the newer parts of the code, but that code is only a few hundred lines.

## Caveats

- **Blame measures current lines, not understanding.** A reviewer who only touched logic, or only reviewed PRs, doesn't show up here.
- **`-M -C` detects moved and copied lines but not every rename.**
- **I did not check how many files import each file.** The skill suggests that for ranking risk, but this library is flat (no subpackages), so I judged it low value.
