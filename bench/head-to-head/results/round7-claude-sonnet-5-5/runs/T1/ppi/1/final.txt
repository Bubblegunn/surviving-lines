### Findings

1. **Troy Goode is the effective sole active owner.** They own 56% of the lines, including 67% of `lib/index.js`, and they committed 2 days before the snapshot.
2. **Wilson's knowledge is mostly historical.** About 33% of the lines are credited to someone who hasn't committed in 5 years, so they are unlikely to be a reviewer.
3. **The long tail is gone.** The other 18 authors hold about 8% of the lines and have been inactive since 2013–2016.
4. **The reformat hides ownership.** Add `62628c0` to a `.git-blame-ignore-revs` file and run `git config blame.ignoreRevsFile .git-blame-ignore-revs`. GitHub's blame view reads that file automatically.
