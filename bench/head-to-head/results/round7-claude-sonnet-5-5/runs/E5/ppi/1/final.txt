## 🔍 Code History: lib/index.js

### Quick Context
> `lib/index.js` is the `cors` middleware (238 lines). Troy Goode wrote it in January 2013 and still owns most of it. Many one-off contributors patched individual CORS edge cases over 13 years.

### Decision Timeline
- **2013-01-30, Troy Goode:** first commit. Same-day fixes made header lookup case-insensitive and made `origin: false` disable CORS.
- **2013-02 to 03:** Goode paused and resumed the request to handle async middleware. After reading the CORS spec, he sent most headers only on the preflight response. Damien Klinnert limited `Access-Control-Allow-Methods` to OPTIONS.
- **2013-04 to 05, Simon Mansfield:** credentials header handling, an early origin check, and handling of an undefined `Origin`.
- **2013-10:** Eric di Domenico added a callback for `config.origin`. Mathias Buus swapped in non-Express request and response methods, so Connect works without Express (v2.1.0).
- **2014:** Typicode added PATCH to the default methods. Alberto Leal fixed the origin callback being overridden. Shane Tomlinson uppercased method names before comparing. Goode added Express 4 support and `Access-Control-Expose-Headers` (v2.3.2 to 2.4.0).
- **2014-10 to 2015:** Igor Soarez added `Vary: Origin` for specific origins, and Goode moved this to the `vary` module. Goode fixed the Express-only regression this caused (#33). Raymond Feng optimized `applyHeaders`. Eran Tiktin stopped `Vary: Origin` from being sent for `*`. max.laverse added passing preflight to `next()`. DracoBlue exposed headers on OPTIONS.
- **2015-05, Alejandro Caravaca Puchades (also as alex94puchades):** regexp and array support for `origin`. Goode moved the file to ESLint the same day.
- **2015-06:** jordandenison fixed a strict-mode problem in the middleware wrapper.
- **2016-08:** Goode fixed #66 and #73 and published 2.8.0.
- **2017, Douglas Christopher Wilson:** he took over maintenance. He added `Vary` for regexp origins (credit to Tomek Kopczuk, who first sent it) and `Vary: Access-Control-Request-Headers`. He fixed a crash when the options delegate had no `methods`. He also did lint upgrades. Benjamin Eidelman added a Safari preflight workaround, and Saad Tazi fixed a frozen-options error.
- **2018 to 2021:** Tom Arad fixed `maxAge: 0`. LuisEGR removed unused parameters.
- **2026-04:** Troy Goode annotated the handlers (`b2005d9`). Fiona Formatter reformatted the file to 4-space indentation and double quotes (`62628c0`).

### Narrative
Goode designed the original structure: an options-normalizing wrapper around a header-applying function. Almost everything since then has been a small compatibility or spec fix from a contributor who hit one edge case. Early work (2013 to 2015) added the main options: callback, regexp and array origins, and exposed headers. Wilson's 2017 changes were mostly about correct `Vary` behavior and robustness. Since 2018 the changes have been tiny.
