`lib/index.js` is the `cors` middleware. It has about 56 commits in its history, and Troy Goode wrote most of it and still owns most of it.

## Who shaped it

**Troy Goode (2013–2016, plus one in 2026).** Troy made the first commit on 2013-01-30 and 29 of the file's commits. In 2013 they added:
- case-insensitive header lookup
- `origin: false` to disable CORS
- request pausing and resuming for async middleware
- the `use strict` pragma
- the JSLint cleanups

In 2014 they added Express 4 support and `Access-Control-Expose-Headers`, and switched to the `vary` module. In 2015 they moved linting to ESLint. Their last big release commit was 2.8.0 in 2016. They also made the recent "annotate handlers" commit (b2005d9, April 2026).

**Contributors who added features:**
- Simon Mansfield (2013) added the early check for an undefined `Origin` and moved where the credentials header is sent.
- Damien Klinnert (2013) limited `Allow-Methods` to preflight (OPTIONS) responses.
- Eric di Domenico (2013) let `origin` be a callback.
- Mathias Buus (2013) switched to non-Express request and response methods, which allowed use with plain Connect.
- Typicode (2014) added PATCH to the default methods.
- Alberto Leal (2014) fixed the `options.origin` callback being overridden.
- Shane Tomlinson (2014) uppercased method names before comparing them.
- Igor Soarez (2014) added `Vary: Origin` for specific origins.
- Raymond Feng (2015) removed the closure in `applyHeaders`.
- Eran Tiktin (2015) fixed `Vary: origin` being added for `*`.
- max.laverse (2015) let the preflight request pass to `next()`.
- DracoBlue (2015) added exposed headers to OPTIONS responses.
- Alejandro Caravaca Puchades, working under two author names (the second is alex94puchades), (2015) added RegExp and array values for `origin`.
- Jordan Denison (2015) added a strict-mode fix.
- Hannah Wolfe (2016) named the anonymous middleware function.

**Douglas Christopher Wilson (2017).** Doug did the maintenance work:
- the `Vary: Access-Control-Request-Headers` header for dynamic `allowedHeaders`
- a fix for options delegates missing `methods`
- the ESLint 2.13.1 upgrade
- paren-spacing lint fixes

Other 2017 changes came from Tomek Kopczuk (`Vary: Origin` for regex origins), Saad Tazi (frozen options), Benjamin Eidelman (a Safari preflight workaround) and Chima Alaebo (removing an unreachable branch).

**Later changes:** Tom Arad fixed `maxAge: 0` in 2018, and LuisEGR removed unused parameters in 2021. After that the file was quiet until April 2026, when Troy's annotation commit and the "Fiona Formatter" reformat (62628c0) landed.
