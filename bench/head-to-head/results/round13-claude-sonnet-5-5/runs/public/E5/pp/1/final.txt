## Who shaped it

**Troy Goode** is the original author and the main shaper. He made the first commit on 2013-01-30 and 29 of the file's commits. In 2013 he added the `origin` callback handling and the JSLint cleanup. He added Connect support without Express and Express 4 support in 2014. He added `Access-Control-Expose-Headers`, switched to the `vary` module, moved to ESLint in 2015, and published 2.8.0 in 2016.

Other contributors, in rough chronological order:
- **2013–2014:**
  - Simon Mansfield added the undefined-`Origin` check.
  - Eric di Domenico allowed `origin` to be a callback.
  - Mathias Buus switched to non-Express request and response methods.
  - Typicode added PATCH to the default methods.
  - Alberto Leal fixed an `options.origin` callback override.
  - Shane Tomlinson normalized method names to uppercase.
  - Igor Soarez added `Vary: Origin` for specific origins.
- **2015:**
  - Raymond Feng removed a closure in `applyHeaders`.
  - Eran Tiktin fixed `Vary` being added for `*`.
  - max.laverse allowed passing the preflight request on to `next()`.
  - DracoBlue added exposed headers to OPTIONS responses.
  - Alejandro Caravaca Puchades, under two identities, added RegExp and array `origin` matching.
  - jordandenison fixed strict-mode handling of non-object options.
- **2016–2017:**
  - Hannah Wolfe named the anonymous middleware function.
  - Saad Tazi fixed a crash on frozen options.
  - Tomek Kopczuk added `Vary: Origin` for regular expressions.
  - Benjamin Eidelman added a Safari pre-flight workaround.
  - Chima Alaebo removed an unreachable branch.
- **Douglas Christopher Wilson** was the maintainer from 2017. He added `Vary: Access-Control-Request-Headers` and fixed the missing-`methods` error in 2017. He also made ESLint and lint fixes.
- **Later:**
  - Tom Arad fixed `maxAge: 0` in 2018.
  - LuisEGR removed unused parameters in 2021.
  - Troy Goode's `lib: annotate handlers` commit (2026-04-10) touched 12 lines.
  - Fiona Formatter did the 2026-04-12 reformat to 4-space indent and double quotes.
