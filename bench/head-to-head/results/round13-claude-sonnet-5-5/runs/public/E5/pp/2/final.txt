`lib/index.js` is the `cors` middleware. It has about 60 commits from 25 authors, and it is 238 lines today. Troy Goode wrote most of it and still owns most of the surviving lines.

**Who shaped it**

  - Callback-style `origin` and Connect support without Express arrived in 2013.
  - `Access-Control-Expose-Headers` and Express 4+ support came in 2014.
