`lib/index.js` is mostly Troy Goode's code. That's the creator and long-time maintainer of the `cors` package, whose history this repo carries. Details below come from `git log --follow` and `git blame -w -M -C`.

**Who shaped it**
- **Troy Goode** made 29 of the roughly 55 commits, from the first ones in April 2013 through 2016, with a few more later.
  - He set up the origin check and the `config.origin` callback.
  - He added the Connect support that works without Express, the Express 4 support, and `Access-Control-Expose-Headers`.
  - He moved to the `vary` module and switched the linting to ESLint.
- **Early contributors (2013–2014):**
  - Simon Mansfield added the checks for a missing `Origin` header.
  - Eric di Domenico let `origin` be a callback.
  - Mathias Buus switched to non-Express request and response methods.
  - Shane Tomlinson normalised method names to uppercase.
  - Typicode added `PATCH` to the default methods.
  - Alberto Leal fixed `options.origin` being overridden.
- **Vary, preflight and origin matching (2014–2015):**
  - Igor Soarez and Eran Tiktin worked on the `Vary: Origin` header.
  - Raymond Feng removed the closure in `applyHeaders`.
  - max.laverse added passing the preflight to `next()`, and DracoBlue added exposed headers on `OPTIONS`.

  - He did ESLint and lint cleanups.
