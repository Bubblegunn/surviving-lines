### Risk assessment

- **Troy Goode owns the core.** `lib/index.js` is 77% Troy's, and Troy is the only active major owner. Troy also committed most recently (April 2026), so this is a concentration risk rather than an abandoned-code risk.
- **Douglas Christopher Wilson owns about a third of the code, mostly in `test/test.js`.** Douglas has been inactive since 2021, so that knowledge is effectively orphaned. It is test code, which lowers the impact.
- **The remaining roughly 11% is spread thinly across about 8 people.** None of them owns more than 3% of the lines.
- **The most recent committers own almost none of the code.** Fiona Formatter, Vansh Sharma, dependabot, Ulises Gascón and others have recent commits but few or no surviving lines. Recent activity is mostly CI, dependency and docs maintenance, not code ownership.

### Recommendations

1. **Immediate:** Have a second person review and co-own `lib/index.js`. Right now only Troy is both active and knowledgeable about it.
2. **Short-term:** Spread `test/vary.js` and the `issue-2`, `error-response` and `example-app` tests beyond Troy.
3. **Ongoing:** Treat Douglas's lines in `test/test.js` as unowned, and document the intent of those tests.

### Caveats

- Only 7 JS files were analyzed. Docs, config and the README were excluded.
- `-M -C` follows moved code, but lines rewritten by the reformat that also changed content may still be credited to the reformatter. I saw no sign of that in the results.
- I didn't check last-commit dates for authors below the top two.
